Community guidelines

A safe, trusted community
for everyone.

Hubro is a moderated community. We take safety seriously so every member feels welcome and protected.

1
Be respectful
Treat every member with kindness and respect regardless of nationality, background, religion or language. No discrimination of any kind.
2
No scams or spam
Do not post fake job offers, housing scams, pyramid schemes or unsolicited advertising. Violations result in immediate removal.
3
Protect personal information
Never share personal documents, bank details or sensitive information in public channels. Use direct messages with trusted contacts only.
4
Share knowledge generously
If you know something that could help another member — share it. This community grows stronger when everyone contributes their experience.
5
Report problems
If you see suspicious activity, scam listings or rule violations, report them immediately to our moderation team. We respond within 2 hours.
6
English in general channels
Please use English in general channels so everyone can participate. Language-specific channels are available for conversations in your native language.
