Privacy Policy

How Hubro collects, uses and protects your personal data

Last updated: 1 June 2025

1. Who we are

Hubro is a trading name of Aevo Group Limited, a company incorporated in Ireland. We operate the platform available at gethubro.app, which connects international residents in Ireland with verified employers, landlords and a trusted community.

Data Controller: Aevo Group Limited, Dublin, Ireland

Contact: info@gethubro.app

We are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What data we collect

Account data

  • Full name and email address
  • Password (encrypted — we never store plain text passwords)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Country of origin and current location
  • Phone number (optional)

Candidate data

  • CV / resume (PDF format)
  • Work experience, skills and education
  • Visa and work permit status
  • Salary expectations and availability
  • Job applications and responses

Employer data

  • Company name, logo and description
  • Job listings and screening questions
  • Subscription and payment information

Landlord data

  • Property details and photos
  • Subscription and payment information

Usage data

  • Pages visited and features used
  • Device type and browser
  • IP address (anonymised)

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing our services — matching candidates with jobs and housing, facilitating applications and enquiries
  • Account management — creating and managing your account, sending verification emails
  • Communications — sending notifications about applications, matches and platform updates
  • AI matching — calculating compatibility scores between candidates and job/housing listings
  • Fraud prevention — verifying listings and users to protect the community
  • Legal obligations — complying with Irish and EU law
  • Platform improvement — analysing usage patterns to improve our services

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide our services to you
  • Legitimate interests — improving our platform, fraud prevention and security
  • Legal obligation — complying with applicable laws
  • Consent — for marketing communications (you can withdraw consent at any time)

5. Who we share your data with

We share your data only with:

  • Employers — your CV and profile when you apply to a job listing
  • Landlords — your profile when you send a housing enquiry
  • Supabase — our database provider, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany)
  • Stripe — our payment processor, for subscription billing
  • Mailchimp — for email communications (waitlist and updates)
  • Google — if you use Google Sign In

All third-party processors are GDPR compliant and process data under strict data processing agreements.

6. Data retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active and for 2 years after deletion
  • CVs and documents — retained for the duration of your account
  • Application data — retained for 2 years
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years (Irish tax law requirement)
  • Usage logs — retained for 90 days

7. Your rights under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to restriction — restrict how we process your data
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@gethubro.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies only — these are required for the platform to function (session management, authentication). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your ability to use certain features of Hubro.

9. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • Regular security reviews
  • Access controls limiting who can access your data

10. International transfers

Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) by Supabase. We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) except where necessary to provide our services (e.g. Stripe for payments, which operates under Standard Contractual Clauses).

11. Children's privacy

Hubro is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at info@gethubro.app.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by displaying a notice on our platform. The date of the most recent update is shown at the top of this page.

13. Contact & complaints

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us:

📧 info@gethubro.app

🏢 Aevo Group Limited, Dublin, Ireland

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) at dataprotection.ie.
