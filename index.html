AI & Technology

Technology that helps people,
not replaces them.

Hubro uses AI to verify CVs, detect fraud, improve matching and personalise recommendations — always with human oversight.

✅ What our technology does

  • ✓ Verifies and scores every CV uploaded to the platform
  • ✓ Matches candidates to relevant jobs and housing listings
  • ✓ Detects fraudulent listings and suspicious profiles automatically
  • ✓ Personalises recommendations based on your full profile
  • ✓ Supports moderation of community content

🚫 What our technology never does

  • ✗ Makes hiring or tenancy decisions alone
  • ✗ Discriminates based on origin, language or background
  • ✗ Replaces human review and oversight
  • ✗ Shares your data without your explicit consent
Important: AI supports decision-making on Hubro but all final decisions involving people are reviewed by our team. We are committed to fairness, transparency and human oversight at every step.

How the 30% threshold works

Every listing shows your real compatibility score so you can focus your energy on applications that matter. Below 30%, the apply button is disabled — protecting both your time and the employer's.

0–29% Cannot apply — does not meet minimum requirements
30–69% Can apply — consider strengthening your CV first
70–89% Strong match — you are a good candidate
90–100% Excellent match — you are among the top candidates
