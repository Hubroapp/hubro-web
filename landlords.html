FAQ

Landlord questions answered.

How quickly will my listing go live? ▼
Once submitted, our AI reviews your listing for completeness and compliance. Most listings go live within 30 minutes. If there are any issues, we will contact you by email with specific guidance.
How are tenants verified? ▼
Every tenant on Hubro completes an identity verification process including government ID check, selfie verification and employment confirmation. Our AI also scans for profile inconsistencies before any profile goes live.
Can I communicate directly with tenants? ▼
Yes — all plans include direct messaging with tenants through the Hubro platform. You can schedule viewings, ask questions and negotiate terms directly. We never share contact details without consent.
What is the match score? ▼
The match score is an AI-generated compatibility percentage between a tenant profile and your property requirements. It considers budget alignment, location preference, tenancy length preference and employment stability. Higher scores indicate better-matched tenants.
Is Hubro GDPR compliant? ▼
Yes. Hubro is operated by Aevo Group Limited, an Irish company fully subject to GDPR. All tenant data is processed with explicit consent, stored on EU servers and never shared with third parties without authorisation.
Can I list multiple properties? ▼
Yes. The Professional plan supports up to 5 properties and the Premium plan supports unlimited listings. You can manage all your properties, enquiries and tenant communications from a single dashboard.
