FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use multiple departments with one account? ▼
Yes — your Hubro account gives you access to all six departments. You can subscribe to plans in any combination. For example, you can be on the Free tier for Employment and the Guided plan for Relocation at the same time.
Can I cancel at any time? ▼
Yes. All monthly plans can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. You keep access until the end of the billing period. One-off Premium Relocation packages are non-refundable once the service has begun.
Is there a discount for annual plans? ▼
Yes — all monthly plans are available at a 20% discount when paid annually. Annual pricing will be displayed during checkout once the platform launches.
How does the 30% match threshold work? ▼
When you apply for a job or a housing listing, our AI calculates your compatibility score based on your profile, skills, experience, and preferences. If your score is below 30%, the apply button is disabled. This protects your time and ensures employers only receive applications from genuinely relevant candidates.
Is my data safe? Is Hubro GDPR compliant? ▼
Yes. Hubro is operated by Aevo Group Limited, an Irish company subject to GDPR. All data is stored on EU servers, encrypted at rest and in transit. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. We never sell your data.
When will Hubro launch? ▼
Hubro is currently in early access. Join the waitlist at gethubro.app and you will be among the first to access the platform when we launch in Ireland. Waitlist members receive 1 month of their chosen plan free at launch.
